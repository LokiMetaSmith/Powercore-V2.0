## Archived: 2026-10-31

* **Repository Context**: Powercore V2.0
  * An open-source wire EDM (electrical discharge machining) power supply developed by Rack Robotics.
  * Consists of CAD models, Firmware (RP2040 Microcontroller), and Motherboard design.
  * Key Specifications: 200 Watts input, 65 VDC output, up to 180 Watts EDM Power Output, 2 KHz EDM cutting.
  * IO Ports: POWER, ALRT (Spark Ignition Telemetry), AMP (Current Sensing Telemetry), EN (PSU Enable Port), USB.
